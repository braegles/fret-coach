# Product brief (draft v1 — Issue #4)

Status: **decided by Braedon, 2026-10-17** (audience, price, voice coach). Only the name is still open (Issue #5).
Working name: Fret Coach (new name in progress, see Issue #5: a practice/coach name, not fret/chord).

## One line
**A guitar coach in your pocket that listens while you practice and tells you exactly what to fix, out loud,
so you keep improving instead of quitting.**

## Who it's for
**Beginners of any age, and anyone who finds it hard to stay motivated to practice.**
They usually practice alone, often from YouTube or TikTok videos, with no teacher to say *what's* wrong or to
keep them going. Motivation is as much the problem as technique, so the product has to feel encouraging,
show progress, and make short daily practice easy to come back to.

**Also:** returning players brushing up, and guitar teachers who want a "homework checker" for students.

## The problem
- Beginners can hear that something sounds bad but not **why**: which string, which finger.
- Videos can't hear you. Teachers cost money every week and aren't there when you practice.
- Existing apps (Yousician, Fender Play, Simply Guitar) mostly score whether you hit the right notes.
  They don't diagnose your hands, and they cost about €20/month.
- Result: frustration and lost motivation in the early weeks, the stage where most beginners give up.
- Practicing alone is easy to skip. Nothing notices your progress or pulls you back the next day.

## The promise
**"Practice with a coach that hears you. Clean chords and smooth changes in 30 days."**
Measurable with the app's own data (clean-check rate going up, chord-change times going down), and that data
is also our best marketing.

## What it is (and isn't)
**Is:** a practice coach. You play, it listens, it coaches, and it tracks your progress. Chords are where it
starts, but the product is *practice + feedback*, not a chord dictionary.

**Isn't (for now):** a song library, a video-lesson platform, or a tab/sheet-music app. Big players own those,
and we'd lose on content.

## Core features (v1 product)
1. **Hears what's wrong and names the fix:** string-by-string and strum checks that point to the exact finger
   (muted, buzzing, wrong fret). *Built.*
2. **Talks you through it:** a voice coach speaks the fix, so your eyes stay on the guitar. One tip at a time,
   with a pause between tips. *Building next (Issue #19).*
3. **A practice plan that keeps you going:** a daily 5-minute workout built from your own mistakes and slowest
   chord changes, with streaks, visible progress and encouragement when you improve. This is the motivation feature. *Habits data built; workout next (Issue #7).*

Supporting: tuner, chord-change trainer, the 8 basic chords (7ths, barre and power are hidden until the basics are polished), installable offline app (all built).
Headline experiment: **camera + sound hand check** (Issue #8, lab prototype).

## Why we win
- **Diagnosis, not scoring:** "your ring finger is muting the D string" beats a red X.
- **Voice coaching** plus (later) **camera** = the closest thing to a teacher in the room.
- **Price:** a fraction of competitors. **Privacy:** audio never leaves the phone.

## Price
| Tier | What's in it | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Tuner, 8 basic chords with full checks, strum check, **voice coach** | $0 |
| Pro | More chords (7ths, barre, power come back once the 8 basics are polished), chord-change loops, daily workout, progress history, future song mode | **$4.99/month** or **$29.99 "founder lifetime"** |

Decided: the voice coach is **free**, so everyone gets the "coach" feeling in their very first session.

## How people find it
Short videos (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) of the app catching real mistakes, "30 days learning guitar" progress series,
before/after chord-change times, and shareable result cards from the app.

## How we'll know it's working
- **Activation:** % of new users who finish their first chord check (target: most of them).
- **Retention:** % who practice again the next day and after 7 days.
- **Conversion:** % of weekly users who go Pro.
- **Learning:** median chord-change time dropping over the first 30 days.

## Decisions (Braedon, 2026-10-17)
1. Audience: **beginners of any age + people who struggle to stay motivated** (not limited to adults).
2. Price: **$4.99/month** or **$29.99 founder lifetime**.
3. Voice coach: **free**.
4. Name: still open (Issue #5, practice/coach direction).
