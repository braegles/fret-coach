Setup

One tip at a time, with a short pause between tips. Plucks are ignored while it talks. Turn your phone's volume up, and make sure the side switch isn't on silent.

Sounds robotic? Your phone has much more natural voices you can download for free: on iPhone go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, pick a voice like Ava, Zoe or Evan, and download the Enhanced or Premium version. Then close and reopen this app and pick it above.

Compare with the "ring" number in the grey line after each pluck. Clean notes should land above this setting and half-muted ones below. Raise it if half-mutes pass; lower it if clean notes fail.

Point the bottom edge of your phone (where the mic is) at the sound hole, 20–40 cm away.
